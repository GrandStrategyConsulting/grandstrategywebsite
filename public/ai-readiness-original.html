Frequently asked questions

Questions L&D leaders ask

Who is the program for?

Organizations seeking to develop foundational AI confidence, practical application and responsible-use habits. The pilot's cohort-based practice groups are designed for up to 12 people.

Do participants need prior AI experience?

No advanced technical experience is assumed. The discovery conversation helps tailor examples and exercises to participants' starting points.

Is this tied to one AI platform?

The core learning approach emphasizes transferable skills such as briefing, judgment, verification and responsible use rather than a single platform.

Can we discuss a different cohort size or delivery format?

Yes. The $9,997 pilot has a defined scope; multiple cohorts, manager enablement, train-the-trainer support and larger programs can be scoped separately.

Can the program align with our AI policies and tools?

Yes. We discuss your approved AI tools, internal policies, risk considerations and relevant work scenarios during scoping. Platform-specific requirements are agreed before delivery.

Can you support teams across locations and time zones?

Delivery for distributed teams can be explored during scoping. Scheduling, facilitation capacity and any localization requirements are agreed as part of the engagement.

How will we assess progress?

The pilot includes a pre-pilot pulse, post-pilot fluency checks, evidence from facilitated practice and a readiness report with recommended next steps. Adoption metrics and extended evaluation can be scoped for larger programs.
